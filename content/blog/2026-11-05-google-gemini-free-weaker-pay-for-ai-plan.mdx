Google's free Gemini app no longer lets you pick Flash or Pro yourself. Google's help page now lists free accounts as "Auto or Flash-Lite," and the footnote is the real rule: with auto selection on, "Most prompts will use Flash-Lite for quick answers. Prompts that require more reasoning may use Flash or Pro." Turn auto off and "all responses will use Flash-Lite" ([Gemini Apps Help](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/18648722?hl=en)).

The calendar date for that free-tier change is not on Google's live help pages today. [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/10/03/gemini-model-limits-oct-26/) reported on Oct 3 that Google's then-updated support doc set Oct 9 as the start for users without a plan, and [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1005451/google-gemini-free-flash-lite-only) repeated that date on Oct 6. On Oct 9, 9to5Google said the free change was rolling out on most of its free accounts ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/10/09/gemini-auto-free-users/)). We're treating Oct 9 as a secondary-sourced date, not a Google page we can quote today.

If you use Gemini for short emails and one-off questions, Flash-Lite is often enough. If you draft proposals, run research, or paste long client files into chat, the paid plans are where the numbers move.

## What each plan actually unlocks

US list prices on Google One's AI plans page: Google AI Plus at $4.99 a month and Google AI Pro at $19.99 a month ([Google One](https://one.google.com/about/google-ai-plans/)). Those are the figures in Google's comparison table headers. Plus also ships as a 2 TB storage option listed at $9.99 a month on Google One's plans page, with the same Flash-model pitch ([Google One plans](https://one.google.com/about/plans)). Prices and availability vary by country. Google's Gemini subscriptions page is the United States view ([Gemini subscriptions](https://gemini.google/us/subscriptions/?hl=en)).

Google's current model table, on the "About Gemini models" help page, not the older limits article, looks like this for chats ([Gemini Apps Help](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/18648722?hl=en)):

- **No plan:** Auto or Flash-Lite (manual Flash and Pro picks are gone)
- **AI Plus:** Flash-Lite, Flash, and Pro
- **AI Pro:** Flash-Lite, Flash, Pro, and Argon
- **AI Ultra:** the same four as Pro, with higher usage

That Plus row still includes Pro on Google's live help page. Oct secondary coverage said Plus would lose Pro after an email. We couldn't verify that loss on Google's pages today, so we're sticking to the live table. Google's marketing pages still sell Plus as Flash access and Pro as Pro plus Argon ([Google One](https://one.google.com/about/google-ai-plans/)). Paid users can pick a model by hand. Free users cannot ([Gemini Apps Help](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/18648722?hl=en)).

Usage multipliers, also from Google: Plus is "2x higher than standard limits," Pro is "4x higher," and Ultra is "5x or 20x higher than AI Pro limits depending on your subscription." Limits refresh every five hours until you hit a weekly cap. Google does not publish a hard prompt count ([Gemini Apps Help](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16275805?hl=en)).

Context windows are the clearest upgrade for anyone uploading files:

| Plan | Context window |
| --- | --- |
| Without an AI plan | 32k tokens |
| AI Plus | 128k tokens |
| AI Pro and AI Ultra | 1 million tokens |

Google's example for the million-token window: "up to 1,500 pages of text or 30,000 lines of code" ([Gemini Apps Help](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16275805?hl=en)).

Storage is bundled. Plus includes 400 GB across Gmail, Drive, and Photos on the $4.99 tier. Pro includes 5 TB ([Google One](https://one.google.com/about/google-ai-plans/)). Free stays at the standard 15 GB Google Account allotment ([Google One plans](https://one.google.com/about/plans)).

## Drafting, research, long docs, images

**Drafting.** Subject lines and short replies sit in Google's "Very low" usage bucket. Long revision threads cost more because Gemini re-reads the chat ([Gemini Apps Help](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16275805?hl=en)). Free Auto will often land you on Flash-Lite for those short asks. For a multi-page proposal you want to steer model by model, Plus or Pro is the path that still lets you pick.

**Deep Research.** Google's feature table marks Deep Research available on free, Plus, Pro, and Ultra. The catch for free accounts: "some features that require more compute resources, like Deep Research, may be unavailable during periods of high demand" ([Gemini Apps Help](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16275805?hl=en)). Google also says Pro and Ultra "get higher limits for how many research reports they can create," without publishing the daily numbers ([Gemini Apps Help](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15719111?hl=en)). Deep Research itself is a "High" usage cost item. If competitor briefs are weekly work, the paid multipliers matter more than the checkbox.

**Long documents and uploads.** This is where Plus and Pro separate cleanly. 32k tokens on free is a short packet of files. 128k on Plus is four times that window. Pro's 1 million tokens is the "paste the whole binder" tier ([Gemini Apps Help](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16275805?hl=en)). If your real job is summarizing contracts, catalogs, or meeting dumps, start the decision at the context column, not the marketing adjectives.

**Image generation.** Image generation with Nano Banana 2 is checked for free through Ultra. "Redo images with Nano Banana Pro" starts at Plus. Video generation also starts at Plus ([Gemini Apps Help](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16275805?hl=en)). For social graphics and product shots, free can generate. For the Pro image redo path and video, you pay.

> Pay when you need a bigger context window or a model you can pick. Stay free when short prompts and Flash-Lite drafts already clear the work.

## A real example Google published

Google's Oct 20, 2026 post on small-business tools names Morgane of Bleu Clémentine, a ceramics studio in Bordeaux. Google's claim: she uses Gemini "to write product descriptions, newsletters, and catchy hooks for social media, saving her two to three hours every day" ([Google blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/new-tools-small-business-owners/)). That's Google's customer story, not our measurement. The workload is drafting and marketing copy, the kind of short-to-medium prompt work free Auto still aims at Flash-Lite for. It does not tell you whether she hits Deep Research caps or a 32k upload wall.

## Illustrative math: Plus vs Pro for a year

US monthly list prices from Google One, multiplied out. No promo assumed.

- Google AI Plus at $4.99 a month: $4.99 × 12 = $59.88 a year ([Google One](https://one.google.com/about/google-ai-plans/)).
- Google AI Pro at $19.99 a month: $19.99 × 12 = $239.88 a year ([Google One](https://one.google.com/about/google-ai-plans/)).
- Difference: $239.88 - $59.88 = $180 a year, or $15 a month, for Pro's 4x usage vs Plus's 2x, the 1M-token window vs 128k, Argon, and the higher Deep Research ceiling Google describes without quantifying ([Gemini Apps Help](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16275805?hl=en)).

Our [under-$150 AI stack post](/blog/small-business-ai-gap-under-150-month) put Workspace Business Standard at $14 per user a month, with Gemini in Gmail, Docs, Meet and more ([Google Workspace pricing](https://workspace.google.com/pricing)). Google's pricing page still lists Business Standard at $14 per user a month, and Gemini AI features are included in Business and Enterprise editions rather than sold as a separate add-on for those plans ([Google Workspace Help](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/generative-ai/workspace-with-gemini/gemini-ai-features-now-included-in-google-workspace-subscriptions), [Google Workspace Help](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/getting-started/editions/business-editions)). Personal Google AI plans are for personal accounts. Workspace customers do not buy Plus or Pro on that same personal path ([Google One](https://one.google.com/about/google-ai-plans/)). If you already pay for Workspace, check what Gemini you already have before stacking a personal plan on the side. Our [surprise AI bill post](/blog/surprise-ai-bill-small-business) is the place to keep caps honest once anything starts metering.

## Pay or stay free

Hypothetical owner patterns, labeled as such:

1. **Stay free.** A few short drafts a day, light image asks, no big file dumps. Auto plus Flash-Lite covers it, and you accept that Deep Research can disappear under load.
2. **Pay for Plus ($4.99/mo US).** You want to pick Flash yourself, you need more than 32k tokens of context, you hit free limits mid-afternoon, or you want Nano Banana Pro redos and video. $59.88 a year is the US list ask.
3. **Pay for Pro ($19.99/mo US).** You regularly upload long documents, want the 1M-token window, want Argon, or Deep Research is weekly work and free or Plus ceilings get in the way. $239.88 a year US list.

Google can change limits without notice. Re-check the [limits page](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16275805?hl=en) and the [model page](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/18648722?hl=en) before you renew on autopilot.
