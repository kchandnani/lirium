If your company pays for Claude Team, your staff got a spreadsheet and a whiteboard last week, and the dashboard builder came out of beta. An owner didn't have to turn any of them on. Anthropic launched Claude Sheets and Claude Whiteboards in beta on Nov. 10, 2026, and moved Claude Dashboards to general availability under a new name, Claude Data. On Team plans, "all three are on by default" ([Anthropic](https://claude.com/resources/articles/sheets-whiteboards-data)).

The same day, Anthropic said partner apps such as Asana, Canva and monday.com can now open as full tools beside a Claude conversation, with Claude making changes inside them ([Anthropic](https://claude.com/resources/articles/interactive-connectors-full-tool)).

That's useful. It also gives Claude new ways to read and change company data. The three built-in tools arrived switched on for Team. The partner apps did not: each one waits until an owner adds it.

## What's beta and what isn't

We checked Anthropic's announcement against its help center. They agree.

- **Claude Sheets: beta.** Available on Pro, Max, Ultra, Team and Enterprise. On by default on Team, off by default on Enterprise ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/18324300-get-started-with-claude-sheets)).
- **Claude Whiteboards: beta.** Same plans, same defaults ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/18324319-get-started-with-claude-whiteboards)).
- **Claude Data: generally available.** Same plans. Two pieces of it, certified queries and live sharing, are still beta. On Team, Data and both beta features are on by default. On Enterprise, the two beta features start off ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/17454700-get-started-with-claude-data)).
- **Free plan: none of the three.** Anthropic's plan comparison marks Data, Sheets and Whiteboards "No" for Free ([Claude pricing](https://claude.com/pricing)).

Enterprise is on a different clock. Sheets and Whiteboards stay off until an owner turns them on, and Claude Data "turns on by default on November 20, 2026, unless an owner has already saved a setting" ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/17454700-get-started-with-claude-data)). That's Friday, and it applies to Enterprise only. Team already has it. The switch lives in Organization settings > Artifacts ([Anthropic](https://claude.com/resources/articles/sheets-whiteboards-data)).

## What it costs

Nothing extra if you're already on Team. Anthropic lists Team at $20 per Standard seat per month billed annually, or $25 billed monthly. Premium seats are $100 annually or $125 monthly ([Claude pricing](https://claude.com/pricing)). Team needs at least two members and tops out at 150 seats, and those are US prices before tax ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9266767-what-is-the-team-plan)).

For a hypothetical eight-person office on Standard seats: 8 × $20 = $160 a month on annual billing, or 8 × $25 = $200 on monthly. Over a year that's $1,920 versus $2,400, a $480 gap. Sheets and Data do count toward your plan's usage limits, like everything else in Claude ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/18324300-get-started-with-claude-sheets)), and on Team those limits apply per member ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9266767-what-is-the-team-plan)).

## Put one tracker in Sheets

Anthropic says Claude can build sheets with tabs, formulas, formatting and drop-downs from a prompt, and outlines each edit it makes so you can follow the changes ([Anthropic](https://claude.com/resources/articles/sheets-whiteboards-data)). Formulas stay in the cells, so you can check how a number was calculated, and version history can roll a sheet back ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/18324300-get-started-with-claude-sheets)).

The help center's limits tell you which tracker to pick. Sheets "can't pull live data from connected apps." There are no pivot tables or protected ranges yet, no macros or VBA, and mobile access is limited. Because it's beta, HIPAA and zero data retention aren't supported ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/18324300-get-started-with-claude-sheets)).

So skip the pipeline report that should come out of your CRM. Start with something your team already updates by hand: next month's staffing grid, or a 13-week cash tracker. Import the current version from Excel, Google Sheets or CSV, share it with "Can edit," and use it for two or three cycles ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/18324300-get-started-with-claude-sheets)). If it outgrows Sheets, export to .xlsx or CSV, or send it to Airtable, Asana, monday.com or Smartsheet ([Anthropic](https://claude.com/resources/articles/sheets-whiteboards-data)). Nothing gets stuck.

## Open the tool you already pay for

Anthropic says the first group of apps that open in full inside Claude includes Asana, Canva, Carta, Dropbox, monday.com, Smartsheet, Vercel and Webflow. Its post also shows Adobe, Atlassian, Box, HubSpot and Notion, and says Figma, Harvey and Shopify are building integrations ([Anthropic](https://claude.com/resources/articles/interactive-connectors-full-tool)).

Anthropic's own HubSpot example fits a small sales team: "Prioritize pipeline tasks and follow-ups, then act on them in Claude." In Box, you can have Claude revise a file and compare the versions before keeping the changes ([Anthropic](https://claude.com/resources/articles/interactive-connectors-full-tool)). Asana's documented example, from an earlier version of its Claude app, shows a team uploading documentation for a global customer initiative and Claude drafting the project for review before a "Create project" step ([Asana](https://asana.com/resources/claude-asana-integration)).

Before you buy another workspace, open the project tool you already use inside Claude for a week. Three conditions, all from Anthropic:

- **It isn't on by default the way Sheets is.** A Team owner has to add each connector, and each person signs in to it individually unless the connector uses a shared credential ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11176164-use-connectors-to-extend-claude-s-capabilities)).
- **You need the new Claude experience.** The full-app view is rolling out on web and desktop on all plans ([Anthropic](https://claude.com/resources/articles/interactive-connectors-full-tool)). On Team, the new experience is available "where Chat and Cowork unified is turned on" ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/16761823-claude-cowork-and-chat-are-one-claude)). It isn't on mobile yet ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13454812-use-interactive-connectors-in-claude)).
- **You use your own account in that tool.** Anthropic says you get the full editor "signed into your own account" ([Anthropic](https://claude.com/resources/articles/interactive-connectors-full-tool)). Claude doesn't replace your Asana or monday.com login.

## What Claude can edit, and what owners can stop

Start with the rule that matters most. Claude "inherits each person's permissions from the connected service." If someone can't open a record in the source system, Claude can't reach it either ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11176164-use-connectors-to-extend-claude-s-capabilities)).

Inside an open app, Claude can change "a page, row, or file that you have open," and you approve its actions the way you approve any connector action ([Anthropic](https://claude.com/resources/articles/interactive-connectors-full-tool)). It can only take actions the connector's developer built ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13454812-use-interactive-connectors-in-claude)).

Owners on Team and Enterprise can narrow that. Each connector's Tool permissions are grouped by type, such as read-only and write/delete, and every category or single tool can be set to Always allow, Needs approval or Blocked. The setting applies org-wide and users can't override it. Anthropic's own example: let Claude read Google Drive files but not create or edit documents ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11176164-use-connectors-to-extend-claude-s-capabilities)). Owners can also turn off just the tool calls that render the in-Claude app view, in Organization settings > Connectors, while text-based tools keep working ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13454812-use-interactive-connectors-in-claude)). Anthropic's launch note adds that if your org already allows all of a connector's tools, the new features appear as they roll out ([Anthropic](https://claude.com/resources/articles/interactive-connectors-full-tool)).

Then there's Claude Data's live sharing, which is on by default for Team:

> Live sharing runs on your access to the data source. Anyone you share the dashboard with sees those results, even if they don't have access to that source.

That's from the [Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/17454700-get-started-with-claude-data). Viewers can't run new queries with your access ([Anthropic](https://claude.com/resources/articles/sheets-whiteboards-data)), but whoever connects your CRM or books decides what everyone else sees. Owners control live sharing and certified queries under Data in Organization settings > Artifacts ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/17454700-get-started-with-claude-data)).
